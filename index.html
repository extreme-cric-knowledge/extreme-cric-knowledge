🏏 Complete Cricket Skills & Strategy Guide

Learn the techniques, skills and thinking behind every part of cricket.

1. 🏏 Batting Skills

Batting stance: Stand balanced with feet comfortably apart, knees slightly bent and eyes on the bowler.

Grip: Hold the bat comfortably with both hands together. Avoid gripping too tightly.

Backlift: Lift the bat before the delivery to prepare for the shot.

Footwork: Move forward for fuller deliveries and back for shorter deliveries. Good footwork helps maintain balance.

Important shots: Straight drive, cover drive, pull, hook, cut, sweep, flick and defensive block.

Timing: Watch the ball carefully and meet it with the middle of the bat.

Running between wickets: Communicate clearly, call early and turn quickly while keeping an eye on the ball.

2. 🎯 Bowling Skills

Grip: Hold the ball comfortably with the fingers positioned according to the bowling style.

Run-up: Build a smooth and controlled approach to the crease.

Line: The direction in which the ball is bowled.

Length: The area where the ball pitches. Common lengths include yorker, full, good length and short-pitched.

Fast bowling: Uses pace, seam movement, swing and changes of speed.

Spin bowling: Uses finger or wrist action to make the ball turn after pitching.

Variations: Slower ball, yorker, bouncer, off-spin, leg-spin, googly and doosra.

Bowling plan: Bowlers try to create pressure by maintaining a consistent line and length and adapting to the batter.

3. 🧤 Fielding Skills

Ground fielding: Stay low, keep your eyes on the ball and stop it with controlled movement.

Catching: Watch the ball until it reaches your hands and use soft hands to control the catch.

Throwing: Aim accurately towards the correct end and communicate with teammates.

Run-outs: Fielders work together to collect the ball and break the stumps before the batter makes their ground.

Field placements: Captains position fielders according to the batter, bowler, match situation and format.

4. 🧤 Wicketkeeping

Stance: Maintain a balanced, crouched position behind the stumps.

Catching: Keep your eyes on the ball and use both gloves to collect it safely.

Stumping: Collect the ball and remove the bails when the batter is outside the crease and not protected by a grounded bat or body.

Communication: Guide fielders, support bowlers and help the captain with observations.

5. 🧠 Match Strategy

Powerplay: A limited-overs phase with special fielding restrictions.

Partnerships: Batters build partnerships by rotating strike and choosing suitable scoring opportunities.

Run rate: Total runs scored divided by overs faced.

Required run rate: Runs still needed divided by overs remaining.

Captaincy: Captains make decisions about bowling changes, field placements and match plans.

Match awareness: Teams adapt their approach according to wickets, overs, pitch conditions and the target.

6. 📊 Important Cricket Statistics

Batting average: Total runs scored divided by the number of times dismissed.

Batting strike rate: (Runs scored ÷ Balls faced) × 100.

Bowling average: Runs conceded divided by wickets taken.

Bowling economy: Runs conceded divided by overs bowled.

Partnership: Runs scored by two batters together before a wicket falls or the innings ends.
